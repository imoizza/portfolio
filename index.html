Design Project 2

Reso

A videoconferencing interface that shows emotion through colour-coded captions and sound-rhythm graphs for Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing users. I led a 5-person team; we validated it with 18 participants.

Svelte · Accessibility · Research

Interactive Machine Learning

A browser-based, client-side sound classifier. Deaf and Hard-of-Hearing users and trusted validators define their own sound classes, with PIN-authenticated labelling and context-based personalisation.

Interactive ML · Svelte · Accessibility
