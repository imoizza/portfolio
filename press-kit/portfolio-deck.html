About

Engineer by training,
designer by conviction

Moizza Azhar

I'm a product designer based in Orsay, France, doing a master's in Human-Computer Interaction at Université Paris-Saclay. I came in through a computer science degree at UET Lahore, which is why I treat engineering constraints as a design input rather than an obstacle.

For two years at SmartEnds I designed 15+ digital platforms, including five client-critical IoT systems monitoring over a thousand devices. That work is where I learned the thing I now care about most: dense, technical data is a design problem, not an excuse.

What I'm good at: taking something complicated and structured, sensor networks, financial flows, multi-step onboarding, and finding the shape that makes it obvious. I build design systems that survive contact with real engineering teams, and prototype early enough that the arguments happen in Figma instead of in production.
